As a remote course, you will often need to share your work in class through digital means. It is important to become comfortable with quickly and effectively capturing physical objects and sketches in a format that you can share in real time.

By the end of this activity, you will be able to:

* Share images of physical objects effectively and quickly in a remote setting.
* Share images of sketches effectively and quickly in a remote setting.
